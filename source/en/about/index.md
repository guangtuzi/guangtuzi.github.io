Welcome to **Mr. Poprocks' Blog**. I am a software developer who believes that continuous learning works best when it leaves a clear trail.

This site captures the decisions, trade-offs, and lessons that emerge from real projects. I care less about declaring that a technology is “best” and more about understanding why it works, where it stops working, and how a team can turn it into repeatable practice.

## What you will find here

- Software engineering: maintainability, testing, refactoring, and developer productivity
- System design: boundaries, data flow, reliability, and performance
- Client and web development: implementation details grounded in user experience
- Open-source practice: tools, automation, and workflows worth keeping

## Writing principles

1. State the problem and constraints before presenting the answer.
2. Record failed paths as well as the final solution.
3. Prefer runnable examples and conclusions that readers can verify.
4. Update old articles when the underlying facts change.
